# Watch forgery and appraisal

Approved user direction: replace one-click disguise with logo repainting, ultraviolet serial forgery and ballast concealing missing parts. Player inspections progressively narrow appraisal, with all three current inspections revealing exact reference market value. Related experts have a high independent chance of knowing that value; ordinary customers have a low chance of approximating it. NPC bids remain negotiable and never mirror private player inspection UI automatically.

Implementation choices: three independent, one-use physical treatments (1 AP each); use existing materials and show their cost and observed before/after readings before confirmation. Base watch identity/authenticity/function/missing parts remain unchanged; physical treatments change displayed logo, UV serial and measured mass, with corresponding tamper clues. Weight treatment cannot remove excess mass. Each buyer checks each applied treatment with its matching logo/serial/structure expertise. A caught treatment permanently records exposure, rejects current selection, adds10 risk and subtracts8 faction relation once per item/buyer encounter; revisits and reloads do not reroll the same encounter.

Exact reference value uses underlying condition, authenticity, function and missing parts, independent of cosmetic treatments and asking price. Each CHECK stores a revision-bound appraisal snapshot. Zero/one/two inspections give nested ranges; all three give exact price. Repair or altered physical evidence makes old estimates stale without deleting evidence; free rechecks refresh each channel. Legacy records remain readable and ask for rechecks; old flat disguises cannot continue pretending the watch was repaired.

NPC professional recognition: related expertise >=80 gets85% chance of an accurate independent valuation; ordinary customers get20% chance of a rough valuation (+/-12%). Misses retain their first-impression valuation. Judgement is cached per encounter/item; private player CHECKs cannot update it. Explicitly showing new evidence updates the buyer's understanding; already established accurate/rough appraisals only refresh when newly disclosed facts are recorded. NPC knowledge never constrains bids to exactly market value.

Clarifications implemented during verification: an exposure record is not a reveal of hidden function/authenticity; each subsequent buyer independently recognizes physical treatments. A buyer who already caught an active treatment refuses repeated sale attempts, including the merchant resale path. Removed ballast remains visible as a repair-history entry. Uninformed first impressions discount visible mismatches; a successful physical treatment can remove that discount, while accurate appraisers keep the true value.
